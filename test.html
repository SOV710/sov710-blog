Two events and are independent if and we write $A ⊥ B$. If they are not independent, we write $A ¬\!⊥ B$.

  • Sometimes we explicitly assume that two events are independent.

  • In other instances, we derive independence by verifying that $(AB) = (A)(B)$.

Important conclusion: Disjoint events with positive probability are not independent.
